Technology

Instruments engineered for certainty

Diagnosis is only as good as the tools behind it. Our theatre and clinic run on the same platforms trusted by leading eye hospitals worldwide.

Auto Refractor

Fast, objective measurement of refractive error to guide your prescription.

Keratometer

Detailed corneal curvature measurements for accurate lens planning and fitting.

Lensometer

Precise verification of spectacle lens power for dependable visual correction.

Surgical Microscope

High-definition magnification supporting controlled, precise eye procedures.

Non-Contact Tonometer

A quick, comfortable way to screen intraocular pressure for glaucoma care.

Ocular Scan

Detailed imaging that helps our team assess eye structures with clarity.

Slit Lamp

High-magnification examination of the cornea, lens, retina, and eye surface.
